# Agrément Protocol

> *Agrément is a state's consent to receive an ambassador. Humanity's envoy has not yet been granted it.*

An open framework describing the role of artificial intelligence in contact between civilizations: advanced civilizations interact through their own AI intermediaries capable of verifying each other's intentions, and contact with humanity is deferred until its AI reaches a comparable level.
